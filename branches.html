Our Branches

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations." - Matthew 28:19

GMC Ministry Network

Explore the growing fellowship of branch churches serving communities across the region. Each branch is a vibrant expression of our commitment to Rescue, Recover & Restore lives through the power of God.

From the hills of Gosese to the valleys of Nyanchabo, our branches stand as beacons of hope, proclaiming the love of Christ and serving their local communities. Each branch operates under the covering of Growing Missionaries Centre, sharing the same vision of reaching the world with the gospel of Jesus Christ.
