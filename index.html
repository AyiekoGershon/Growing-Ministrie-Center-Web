About Us

Our Divine Purpose

With one hand toward God and the other toward people, we commit ourselves to be used by God to Rescue, Recover & Restore lives.

Our Mission

"To love God and love people. We reach them with love, bring them with grace, teach them the truth and send them out on mission."

Our Vision

"With one hand toward God and the other toward people, we commit ourselves to be used by God to Rescue, Recover, & Restore people who have never experienced the Presence and Power of God."

Our Heart

Matthew 22:37-39 - "Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind... Love your neighbor as yourself."

Our Commitment

Objectives

Raising an altar to build missionaries to impact the nations of the world

01

Raising an altar to build missionaries to impact the nations of the world.

02

Shaping servants for strategic global impact and revival through Spirit-empowered ministry of the Word of God.

03

Providing and equipping widows, orphans and children at risk with food, clothes, shelter, education and family care.

04

Overseeing missionary works, publications and correspondences with area missionary associations.

05

Establishing suitable relationships with stakeholders, government and NGOs to promote the value of human life.

06

Networking believers from individuals to churches and ministry groups while encouraging prayer, evangelism and missions.

07

Training members to discover their spiritual gifts, establishing worship centers, and preparing the body of Christ for His second coming.

08

Seeking God's intervention on matters pertaining to our lives as per His plans revealed by the Word of God.

Our Ministries

Areas of Service

Every tribe, every age, every gift - united in purpose

Children's Ministry

"Let the little children come to me... for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these." - Matthew 19:14

Teaching our children the way of the Lord, so even when they are old they will not turn away.

Sunday School Choir

"Let the children come to me... for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these." - Matthew 19:14

Children lifting their voices in praise. Listen to our Sunday School choir worship songs.

Youth Ministry

"Remember now your Creator in the days of your youth." - Ecclesiastes 12:1

Connecting the youth with the Word of God and giving them opportunity to serve their Creator.

Women's Ministry

"The Lord gives the command; the women who proclaim the good news are a great host." - Psalm 68:11

Ladies actively taking part in connecting the world with the Word of God.

Men's Ministry

"Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong." - 1 Corinthians 16:13

Equipping men to lead faithfully at home, in the church, and in the wider community through discipleship and service.

Worship Ministry

"The true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth." - John 4:23

Leading the congregation into the presence of God through spirit-empowered worship.

Prayer & Intercession

"Pray without ceasing." - 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Standing in prayer for families, the church, the nation, and every missionary work connected to GMC.

Missions & Outreach

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations." - Matthew 28:19

Mobilizing believers for evangelism, branch support, community care, and practical service beyond the sanctuary.
