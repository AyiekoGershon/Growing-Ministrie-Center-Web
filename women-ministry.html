Ministry Focus

Women's Ministry

"The Lord gives the command; the women who proclaim the good news are a great host." - Psalm 68:11

Women Active in Faith and Service

Our Women's Ministry empowers women of all ages to grow in their faith, build meaningful relationships, and become bold witnesses for Christ. We create a space where women are valued, equipped, and inspired to live out their calling with courage and purpose.

Our Vision

We believe women are essential to God's kingdom. Our vision is to cultivate a community of women who are deeply connected to God's word, supportive of one another, and actively engaged in proclaiming the good news to their families, workplaces, and communities.

"For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many." - Mark 10:45

What We Offer

Women's Fellowship

Monthly gatherings for worship, study, and deep fellowship where women connect and encourage one another in faith.

Bible Study Groups

In-depth Bible study sessions exploring God's word and its application to our daily lives and challenges.

Mentorship Programs

Mentoring relationships that provide guidance, support, and accountability for spiritual growth and life purpose.

Community Outreach

Service initiatives focused on helping vulnerable women, caring for orphans and widows, and community development.

Leadership Development

Training and equipping women to step into leadership roles in the church and their communities with confidence.

Special Events

Retreats, conferences, and celebrations that refresh, inspire, and strengthen the bonds of sisterhood in Christ.

How to Join Us

Whether you're a teenager, young adult, mother, or grandmother, there's a place for you in our Women's Ministry. We meet regularly for worship, study, and service. Every woman is valued and has unique gifts to contribute to our ministry. Come experience the joy of walking with God alongside a community of supportive sisters.

Join Our Community

Be part of a sisterhood dedicated to faith, growth, and making a difference in the world through God's love.
