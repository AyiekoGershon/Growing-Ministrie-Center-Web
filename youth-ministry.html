Ministry Focus

Youth Ministry

"Remember now your Creator in the days of your youth." - Ecclesiastes 12:1

Empowering Young Adults to Live Boldly for Christ

Our Youth Ministry is dedicated to connecting young people with Jesus, equipping them with biblical wisdom, and inspiring them to be agents of change in their generation. We create opportunities for youth to grow spiritually, build meaningful relationships, and discover their purpose in God's kingdom.

Our Vision

We believe the youth are the future of the church and society. Our vision is to raise a generation of young believers who are deeply rooted in God's word, passionate about their faith, and actively engaged in serving their communities. We want every young person to experience the transformative power of knowing Christ personally.

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity." - 1 Timothy 4:12

What We Offer

Youth Fellowship

Weekly gatherings where youth connect, worship, learn God's word, and strengthen their faith together in a vibrant community.

Biblical Teaching

Deep dives into Scripture that address real-life issues young people face - relationships, purpose, identity, and values.

Mentorship & Discipleship

Connecting youth with mature believers who provide guidance, support, and spiritual accountability in their faith journey.

Service & Outreach

Hands-on opportunities to serve in the community, mission trips, and outreach programs that extend God's compassion.

Social Activities

Fun events, camps, retreats, and gatherings that build lasting friendships and create joyful memories together.

Leadership Development

Training and opportunities for youth to develop their talents, discover their spiritual gifts, and take leadership roles.

How to Participate

Our Youth Ministry meets on Wednesday evenings at 7:00 PM for engaging fellowship, worship, and biblical instruction. We also organize special retreats, mission trips, and community service projects throughout the year. Whether you're new to faith or have walked with God for years, you'll find a welcoming community where you can grow spiritually and discover your calling.

Ready to Join Us?

Connect with fellow young believers and embark on a journey of faith, friendship, and purpose. We'd love to see you!
