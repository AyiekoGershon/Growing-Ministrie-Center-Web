Ministry Focus

Prayer & Intercession

"Pray without ceasing." - 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Standing in Prayer for the Nations

Our Prayer & Intercession Ministry is dedicated to prayer as the most powerful tool in God's hands. We believe that prayer changes things, moves mountains, and transforms lives. Through unified, fervent intercession, we stand in the gap for families, our church, our nation, and every missionary work connected to GMC.

Our Vision

We envision a community of believers who are passionate intercessors, standing in prayer for revival, healing, and redemption. Our goal is to create a culture of prayer where believers understand their privilege and responsibility to intercede for others, where prayer is not just an activity but a lifestyle, and where heaven and earth are connected through the power of believing prayer.

"The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective." - James 5:16

What We Offer

Corporate Prayer Meetings

Regular gatherings for united, fervent prayer where believers lift up concerns and intercede for specific needs.

Intercessory Prayer for Nations

Strategic prayer for political leaders, government, justice, and revival in Kenya and nations around the world.

Prayer for the Church

Ongoing intercession for God's church, our pastor, leadership, members, and the spreading of the Gospel.

Prayer for Healing

Dedicated prayer ministry for the sick, suffering, and those in need of God's healing touch and restoration.

Prayer & Fasting

Teaching on the power of prayer combined with fasting and guidance for seasons of deep spiritual intercession.

Prayer Training

Equipping believers with biblical foundations and practical strategies for effective, fervent prayer.

How to Participate

Whether you're a seasoned intercessor or just beginning your prayer journey, you have a vital role in our Prayer Ministry. We meet regularly for prayer meetings and welcome all believers who desire to stand in intercession. Prayer is one of the most powerful gifts you can offer—your prayers change lives and influence kingdoms.

Join the Prayer Warriors

Use the power of prayer to intercede for families, our church, our nation, and missionaries across the world.
