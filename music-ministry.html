Music Ministry

Sayuni Harvesters Choir

"Make a joyful noise to the Lord, all the earth!" - Psalm 100:1

Carrying the Sound of GMC Through Praise & Testimony

The Sayuni Harvesters Choir is a worship team carrying the sound of Growing Missionaries Centre through praise, testimony, and gospel music that points people back to Christ. Through spirit-empowered worship, we create an atmosphere where the Holy Spirit moves and lives are transformed.

Photo Gallery

Our Vision

We envision a worshiping community where every heart is attuned to the presence of God, where believers encounter the living Christ, and where lives are transformed by the power of authentic worship expressed through music and song. Our goal is to create an environment where the Holy Spirit is honored, welcomed, and given freedom to work in the hearts of those who gather.

"Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth. Sing to the Lord, praise his name; proclaim his salvation day after day." - Psalm 96:1-2

What We Offer

Sunday Worship Leading

Leading the congregation into God's presence during Sunday morning services through spirit-filled worship music.

Choir Performances

Presenting gospel music, anthems, and special presentations that uplift and inspire the body of Christ.

Worship Training & Discipleship

Equipping worship leaders, singers, and musicians with biblical foundations and musical excellence.

Songwriting & Composition

Creating original worship songs that express our faith, testimony, and encounter with God's presence.

Recordings & Media

Producing worship recordings and media content that spreads the message of GMC beyond our walls.

Special Events & Concerts

Hosting worship nights, gospel concerts, and special musical events that draw people into deeper worship.

Rehearsal Schedule

The Sayuni Harvesters Choir meets for rehearsals every Thursday at 6:30 PM. We welcome new members who have a heart for worship and a desire to use their musical gifts for God's glory. No matter your skill level, if you have a passion for worship music, there is a place for you.

Join the Sayuni Harvesters Choir

Use your musical talents to lead others into the presence of God and be part of a community that worships with passion and purpose.
